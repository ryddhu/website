The short version

Nubbito is a video app for young children. A parent sets it up and decides what their child can watch. The videos come from YouTube, not from us. Our safety filter checks every video before your child sees it, but no filter is perfect, and Nubbito does not replace your supervision. The app is free, we collect nothing about your child, and you can stop using it at any time by uninstalling it.

The rest of this page says the same things more carefully.

2. What Nubbito is

Nubbito presents a filtered selection of YouTube videos to children. Before a video is shown, it is assessed by an on-device safety filter that examines the title, description, channel and thumbnail. A parent sets how strict that assessment is, may add their own blocked words, and may set daily watch limits. These controls are protected by a PIN.

Nubbito is not a general-purpose YouTube browser, and it is not YouTube Kids or any other Google product.

3. Who may use Nubbito

Nubbito is designed for children to use, but only an adult may install and configure it. By setting up the app you confirm that you are the parent or legal guardian of the child who will use it, or that you have that person's permission, and that you are at least 18 years old.

There are no accounts and no sign-in. Anyone with physical access to the device and the PIN can change the settings — so choose a PIN your child cannot guess, and treat it the way you would any other parental control.

5. What the safety filter can and cannot do

This is the most important section on this page, so we have written it plainly.

What it does. Every video is scored against several layers before your child sees it: keyword matching across 15 Indian languages in native and romanised script, a behavioural-risk assessment, publisher reputation, and on-device analysis of the thumbnail image. Videos scoring below your chosen threshold are not shown.

What it cannot do. The filter reads what a video says about itself — its title, description, channel and thumbnail. It cannot watch the video. A video that is described innocently and turns unsuitable partway through can still reach your child. Advertisements are not screened at all. Newly published videos may be assessed on limited information.

So: Nubbito reduces risk. It does not eliminate it, and we do not promise that it does. It is a tool to support your supervision, not a substitute for it. If something unsuitable gets through, please tell us at support@ryddhu.com — reports are how the filter improves.
